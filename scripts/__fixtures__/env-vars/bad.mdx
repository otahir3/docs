---
title: Bad env vars
---

Set `API_BASE_URL` and `PROJECT_ID` in your server environment.

Then read them: `process.env.API_BASE_URL` and `process.env.PROJECT_ID`.

These are fine: `MARKETBOX_API_URL`, `MARKETBOX_PROJECT_ID`, `MARKETBOX_API_KEY`,
`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_OIDC_CLIENT_ID`.
