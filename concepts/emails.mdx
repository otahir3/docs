title: Emails
description: What MarketBox sends on your behalf, when, and what you control.

MarketBox sends transactional email automatically as bookings are created, changed and completed.
You do not trigger these — creating a booking through the API is enough. This page covers every
email that exists, who receives it, and which parts you can configure.

Emails are sent from MarketBox's infrastructure under your project's name. Nothing here is a
marketing surface: there is no list, no unsubscribe funnel, and no campaign. Every message is tied
to one booking or one invitation.

## The full set

<Note>
All times are relative to the booking. "Start" and "end" mean the booking's scheduled start and end,
not when it was created.
</Note>

### When a booking is created

| Email | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | the client | immediately |
| New booking | the assigned supplier | immediately |

Both are sent as part of the create call. A booking created without a supplier assigned sends only
the client's.

**A hold sends nothing — converting it is what sends these.** A hold only claims a supplier's time;
no booking exists yet, so there is nobody to confirm anything to. `POST /holds/{reservationId}/convert`
is the moment the booking is created, so that is when the client confirmation and the supplier notice
go out, along with everything scheduled below. If your checkout holds time while the client pays, the
client is mailed when your payment webhook converts — not when they reach the payment page. A
duplicate webhook delivery that replays the same bookings does not re-send them. See
[Hold slots during checkout](/guides/hold-slots-for-checkout).

### Before it happens

| Email | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reminder | the client | 24 hours before start |
| Daily agenda | each supplier | 07:00 in the supplier's own timezone |

The agenda lists that supplier's bookings for the day and is the only recurring email in the system.
Suppliers with nothing booked get nothing.

### When something changes

| Email | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking updated | client, current supplier, previously assigned supplier, project admins | on a change to time, location or supplier |
| Booking cancelled | client, supplier, project admins | on cancellation |

Cancelling a booking also removes every email still scheduled for it — no reminder, no rating
request afterwards.

### After it happens

| Email | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leave a rating | the client | 1 hour after end |
| Rating follow-up | the client | 3, 6 and 9 days after end |
| Rate your client | the supplier | 1 hour after end |
| Rating follow-up | the supplier | 3, 6 and 9 days after end |

Both sides are asked independently: the client is asked to rate the supplier, and the supplier to
rate the client and confirm they attended. Neither knows about the other.

### Account

| Email | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation | the invited supplier or staff member | when an invitation is created or resent |

## Stopping the rating follow-ups

The follow-ups exist to chase a rating that never arrived. **They stop as soon as that side leaves
one** — a client who rates after the first email never receives the other three.

What makes that work is the review being linked to the booking. Reviews created through
`POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/bookings/{bookingId}/reviews` are linked by definition, so
suppression is automatic.

<Warning>
A review created through the older project-level `POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/reviews` route
**without** a `bookingId` is not linked to any booking, so MarketBox cannot tell it happened and the
client keeps being asked. If you use that route, always supply `bookingId`.
</Warning>

The two directions are tracked separately. A supplier rating a client does not stop the client's
follow-ups, and vice versa — each side is only ever chased about its own missing rating.

See [Reviews and reports](/resources/reviews) for the endpoints themselves.

## Where the rating links go

The rating emails contain five star links and a button. By default they lead to a MarketBox-hosted
page where the recipient can leave their rating and confirm attendance — no login, and nothing for
you to build.

If you would rather collect ratings on your own site, set a **review redirect URL** in your
project's notification settings in the MarketBox dashboard. Every rating link then points there
instead, with the booking identified in the query string:

```
https://yourdomain.com/review?bookingId=bk_01kv60qge5eh59ap0gw0pqrrys&role=client&rating=4
```

- `bookingId` — always appended, whatever URL you configure
- `role` — `client` or `supplier`, so one page can serve both
- `rating` — present only on the star links, carrying the star that was clicked

You can also template the path — `https://yourdomain.com/review/{bookingId}` substitutes the id and
still appends the query parameter, so either style works.

<Tip>
Ignore query parameters you do not recognise. More may be added, and a page that validates strictly
will break when they are.
</Tip>

Your page then posts to the API from your own server, using your API key. See
[Reviews and reports](/resources/reviews).

## Sender identity

Two settings, both in your project's notification settings in the dashboard:

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| From name | The display name on every email from this project. Defaults to the project name. |
| Reply-to address | Where replies go. Unset means replies go to the platform default. |

Neither is stored on the email itself — they are resolved when each message is sent, so renaming
your project changes the name on the next send, including for bookings made before the change.

## Limits worth knowing

- **There is no per-project switch to turn emails off.** Every project on MarketBox sends the full
  set.
- **Nothing marks a booking complete automatically.** The rating emails fire on the booking's
  scheduled end time, not on a status change, so they arrive whether or not anyone updated the
  booking afterwards.
- **Cancelled bookings send nothing further**, but a booking marked as a no-show still stops the
  rating requests too.
- **Emails cannot be resent through the API.** Invitations are the exception — resending one sends a
  fresh email.
